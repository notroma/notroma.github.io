How Was This Built

The Idea

  • To write, to share important things.
  • To do so from my experience and from love.
  • To do it personally and casually. Nbd.

The Vision

I woke up with a vision of how to do this - the Notes app. What if I could share all of my Notes app with you? And you could see this anytime you wanted?

You get to flip through my Notes, at your pleasure.

A website, and this interface just fit perfectly. The design is inspired by Apple Notes - Personal, yet casual. Organized, yet scattered. Familiar, yet curious.

How I Built It

roma.com was too expensive, so I bought notroma.com

Creating this website happened incredibly fast, it took maybe four or five hours from concept to completion with ChatGPT and Claude.

I gave the idea and a screenshot of the Notes app to o3-mini-high and I asked it to make it with HTML and CSS. o3-mini-high gave me a base template with three columns (for the Desktop version).

I iterated a bit, then I got Claude Sonnet 3.7 involved for the next part of the CSS with nested folders and responsive design (for mobile and tablet). Claude the OG almost one-shot everything with transitions and animations. Here are my chats with the prompts:

o3-mini-high - Base Design
Sonnet 3.7 - Nested Folders, Responsive Design

I spent some time discussing the best way to organize the architecture. Where would I store the data? How would I host it? Should I look into static site generators like Jekyll or Hugo? After some discussion, I decided Github Pages was the easiest option for me

I then redirected my domain to my Github Pages and voila :)
