Who am I?


This is a dump of notes, unedited and straight from my mind. Will edit when I feel like it.

Reduce dimensionality -> happiness

The more dimensions we identify ourselves with -> more it takes for us to be happy.

For eg: I need $1 million and then, I'll be happy. I need a fit body and then, I'll be happy. I need someone to love me, and then I'll be happy.

Imagine these as columns on a spreadsheet. There are values under each of these columns. I'm justifying a contract with myself that I'll be happy only when these values reach my target.

Instead, maybe wondering why these columns even exist - seems to a lot more meaningful. Sometimes, looking at a column and just wondering makes the column disappear -> I have successfully reduced dimensionality. I'm free-er to be happy now, no more columns. I don't need money to be happy, I don't need a fit body to be happy.

I can be anything, anywhere. I am free to move across dimensions.

I am not defined by anything.

How do I identify these columns?

Good indicator are things I am most scared of -> defined by columns, definition of myself

I am alive -> fear of death??

Float like a butterfly.

Let yourself be afraid. Feel the loss. Losing identity is a completely different skillset to thinking/analysing. In fact, losing identity puts you in your body, lets you feel things, and accept it's. It's about allowing.

Allow your whole self to emerge, discover not focus.

Accept everything, be happy.
