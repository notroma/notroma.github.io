How you treat others is how you treat yourself

How you treat others <-> How you treat yourself

This is my gut feeling, and something I've seen play out within myself many many times.

Any time I've been angry at someone for something that's not really their fault, withdraw from someone, or dismiss someone - I've noticed that the voice in my head (a part of me), does the same thing to me when I'm in the same situation. The rules seem to play out pretty symmetrically.

Any time I see someone reacting a particular way - somewhere I know that's exactly how they treat themselves too. That's how the voice in their head treats them too.

This concept has been helpful for me notice a few things about myself.

If someone is truly happy for others, they seem to be truly happy for themselves too.

Anyway, this is a vague sense. I'll update this when I have a better form of this feeling.
